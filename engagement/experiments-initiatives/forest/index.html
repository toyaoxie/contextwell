Experiment & Initiative

Forest / 留林

An art and ecological archive exploring how we see, remember and relate to the forests around us.

If the forest disappeared, what would you keep?

如果森林消失，你想留下什么？

The idea

Forest / 留林 begins in Xishuangbanna, where forests are part of everyday life, ecological systems and local memory. The project uses photography, painting and Chinese calligraphy to turn encounters with forest into a small, evolving archive.

The space

The first works will be created through repeated visits to natural spaces and exhibited in an everyday setting rather than a conventional gallery. A small family rice-noodle restaurant in Jinghong becomes a point of encounter between forest, art and daily life.

A living archive

From forest to everyday life, and back again.

Encounter

Reflect

Carry attention back to the place.

First edition

01 — What Would You Keep?

The first edition follows two siblings into the forests and natural spaces around Jinghong. Photography records what is seen. Oil painting interprets what is felt. Calligraphy asks what deserves to be remembered. The works remain on view in the family restaurant as a small, continuing exhibition and archive.

The project is not intended to speak for conservation science. It creates a cultural and spatial interface through which scientific knowledge, local memory, personal attention and everyday encounters may meet.

What it may become

A small experiment with room to grow.

Forest / 留林 is deliberately starting small. Over time, the archive may develop through new field sites, artists, community contributions, exhibitions, collectors and conservation partners. Its direction will be shaped by what the encounters make possible.
