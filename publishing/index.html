The Passage

A bilingual poetry collection moving between dreams and waking life, where ordinary things open onto the unknown.

For those moving through solitude, uncertainty, or the feeling of being lost. These poems offer no easy answers. They make a little room to stay, to listen, and to find the world still waiting on the other side.

The imprint

Different forms.
Shared questions.

ContextWell Press publishes books and original work that bring ideas about human experience and well-being into everyday life.

Part of ContextWell, the press has its own space for creative and intellectual work. Some ideas begin in research; others emerge through writing, observation and lived experience. Each deserves a form suited to the work, and room for readers to make their own connections.

Our interests meet at a shared question: what helps people and societies live well?
