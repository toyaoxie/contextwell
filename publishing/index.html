那一道缝｜The Passage

A bilingual poetry collection moving between dreams and waking life. Through quiet, symbolic encounters with the ordinary and the unknown, it offers companionship to readers navigating loneliness, uncertainty, or a sense of being lost.

The poems are written in Chinese and English, with each English version shaped as a literary work in its own right.
